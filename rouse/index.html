Rouse

The alarm for your meetings.

Notifications get ignored. Rouse takes over the screen — phone locked, in your pocket, or mid-app — so you never miss a Google Calendar meeting again.

What Rouse does

Full-screen alarmsA popup with a live countdown fires 15, 5 and 1 minute before every meeting — over the lock screen, with sound and vibration.
Your calendar & tasksConnect your Google account to see meetings and Google Tasks, create or edit them, and join Google Meet in one tap.
Built for real phonesEngineered to survive aggressive battery managers on vivo, OPPO, Xiaomi and more — with a built-in locked-screen test.
Private by designNo servers. Your calendar and task data is fetched directly from Google and stays on your device. Nothing is collected, shared or sold.

Your data

Rouse requests access to your Google Calendar and Google Tasks solely to show your schedule and fire reminders on your device. Rouse's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read the full Privacy Policy.
