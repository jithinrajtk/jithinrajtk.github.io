When to call — quick guide

Note: KSFE deducts 5% commission from each winner. Dividend you receive may be taxable.

Free KSFE chitty calculator for Kerala members. Enter your SMS amount — get an instant answer on whether to call this month, your exact loss, and how it compares to a personal loan.

SMS amount

The amount KSFE credits monthly equals your installment minus your share of the winning bid. A higher SMS amount means less competition — a better time to call.

Loss percentage

Your loss is the winning bid plus KSFE's 5% commission. Below 10% is excellent. Above 20% means high competition — better to wait for a quieter month.

Chitty vs personal loan

At low competition, calling a chitty is usually cheaper than a personal loan at 15–18% annual interest. The calculator shows both options side by side so you can decide.

Supported schemes

Works for any KSFE chitty from ₹1 lakh to ₹10 lakh, across durations of 20 to 60 months. Adjustable winners per month, lucky winners, and commission rate.
